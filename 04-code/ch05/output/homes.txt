WHAT DOES NOT BEND
  Product vendor      the product
  Systems integrator  the customer's platform
  Model lab           the model
HOW THE EMPLOYER IS PAID
  Product vendor      subscription, plus a fixed or capped services fee
  Systems integrator  billable hours or a fixed-price statement of work
  Model lab           a usage commitment and consumption
WHO PAYS AN INTEGRATION HOUR
  Product vendor      the vendor's margin, recovered at renewal
  Systems integrator  the customer, by the hour
  Model lab           a partner, billed to the customer; the lab funds the first slice
WHERE REUSE GOES
  Product vendor      product backlog
  Systems integrator  method library and accelerators
  Model lab           research feedback, evals and reference implementations
WHAT CLOSES A REUSE ITEM
  Product vendor      a released feature used by a second customer
  Systems integrator  an asset reused on a second engagement, with the client's IP removed
  Model lab           an eval case or failure accepted by the model team
HOW IT ENDS
  Product vendor      Live Stable gate, then the vendor's product support
  Systems integrator  contract end, or a run-state managed service
  Model lab           hand-over to the customer's team or a partner after the first use case
WHAT THE FDE IS MEASURED ON
  Product vendor      time to production and renewal
  Systems integrator  utilization, margin per hour and follow-on work
  Model lab           consumption, flagship deployments and model feedback
ONE MORE MONTH OF ENGAGEMENT
  Product vendor      costs the employer
  Systems integrator  earns the employer more
  Model lab           costs the employer
